# R09: robust disclosure orders under shared constraints

Research date: September 19, 2026. Predecessor: R08 at commit 254f5d9.

Keep the question stable: for sequential hard-evidence games under jointly
constrained uncertainty, when does one disclosure order sustain the target in
every compatible model, and what uncertainty information determines enforcement?

Start with R08's independent-bit, complementary-evidence benchmark. Preserve
positive costs, favorable ties, known actual law for players, one model-independent
institutional order, and a compact probability family inside (0,A/(A+B))^n.
A polytope constrains model parameters, not the within-model independent bits.

Attack vertex-only testing, greedy selection, pairwise summaries and adaptive
ordering before proposing general theorems. Seek exact general-n characterizations
and finite checkable certificates; do not infer hardness from factorial enumeration.
Literature should include convex alternatives, scheduling, reliability, Boolean
functions and combinatorial set systems, not just signaling.

R08 retains an experiment summary but no executable source for its reported 4,802
checks. This run will build new standalone exact checks, not claim to replay that
missing historical implementation. No prior records will be overwritten.
