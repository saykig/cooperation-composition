# Conjectures killed, narrowed or deferred

- **Check only polytope vertices:** false with three senders and two vertices (C1).
  Replace it by a weighted log-product tangent certificate (T2).
- **Full pairwise information always fails / always suffices:** both overclaims.
  It suffices at n=3, can fail at n=4; T4 generalizes the precise boundary.
- **Largest cost/reward first:** false (C2). Stable r_i p_i ranking yields a valid
  restricted theorem; arbitrary cost sorting does not.
- **Successful permutations form a poset/antimatroid/greedoid language:** false (C3).
- **An adjacent dominance rule proves global partial-order optimization:** not
  established. Moving across incomparable senders changes their suffixes. An early
  draft assertion was removed before retention of that theorem file; only safe
  adjacent swaps and a complete stable-score ranking are proved.
- **Adaptive ordering must improve a strategic cascade:** false for deterministic
  public ordering in the AND benchmark (T6). Hidden future randomization is open.
- **Convexify institutional order choices and exchange them with nature:** invalid.
  The minimax theorem applies only to constraint weights at a FIXED order. The
  two-sender diagnostic preserves the prior quantifier warning.
- **Factorial search means NP-hardness:** invalid. T8 proves NP membership for
  rational segments; no hardness reduction has been established.
- **Numerical convex optimization settles exact zero margins:** invalid. Favorable
  ties make boundary equality decisive. Rational certificates are checked exactly;
  the general real certificate is not automatically polynomial-bit rational.
- **Everything is a standard geometric program:** too strong for an arbitrary
  signed H-polytope. Concavity in the original probabilities is enough.
- **A new general enforcement theory is needed:** not supported. Minimax, concavity,
  polyhedral normals and univariate algebra supply most of the machinery.
- **R08's 4,802 checks were reproduced:** unsupported; source files are absent from
  its retained phase. This run supplies independent new code and separate receipts.
