## Quantifiers and rejected overstatements

Robustness means one order/fine such that, for every compatible known-to-players p,
there exists a sequential equilibrium realizing on-path silence and C. Equilibrium
strategies may depend on p; the institutional order/fine may not. This is neither
one common strategy profile nor success in every equilibrium.

* “A weak blocker makes every equilibrium silent” is false: the exact two-sender
  mixed example in `math/AUDIT.md` §7 has silent and disclosing equilibria.
* On-path incentives alone do not prove sequential equilibrium. The primitive
  checker rejects an all-silent plan with a noncredible off-path completion threat.
* Off-path beliefs cannot be selected arbitrarily: this game has unique consistent
  beliefs. Fully mixed approximating profiles need not themselves be equilibria.
* Correlated bits, extra private signals, zero costs, priors at/above τ, negative
  evidence and changed fine incidence remain outside this audit.

The central question stays unchanged. The next bounded confidence step is a formal
statement of the unique-belief lemma and the continuation bridge, then the exact
target-existence theorem. No growing-dimension work began during this gate.
