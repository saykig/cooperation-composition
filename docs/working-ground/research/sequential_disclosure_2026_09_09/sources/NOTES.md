# Literature and novelty notes — September 9, 2026

This is a targeted comparison, not a systematic review.

## Closest foundations

- Dughmi, Niazadeh, Psomas and Weinberg, *Persuasion and Incentives Through the
  Lens of Duality* (2019): minimum nonnegative payments supporting a fixed
  signaling scheme are already explicit.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.10584
- Ozdogan and Saglam, *Correlated equilibrium under costly disobedience* (2021):
  a cost of departing from recommendations is already a direct object.
  https://mpra.ub.uni-muenchen.de/99370/1/MPRA_paper_99370.pdf
- Gentzkow and Kamenica, *Bayesian persuasion with multiple senders and rich
  signal spaces* (2017): multiple senders and correlated signals.
  https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0899825617300817
- Li and Norman, *On Bayesian persuasion with multiple senders* (2018): correlation,
  timing and equilibrium restrictions in multi-sender persuasion.
  https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0165176518301964
- Teja, Ghalme and Gujar, *Multi-Sender Bayesian Persuasion with Imperfect
  Information* (2026): locally informed senders and product-posterior structure.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08675
- Arieli, Gradwohl and Zeng, *Decentralized Persuasion*: decentralized information
  and public randomization. Only author/EC materials were verified in the
  originating audit; the full paper was not obtained there.
  https://sites.google.com/view/yishuzeng/home/research
- Li and Norman, *Sequential persuasion* (2021): sequential communication and
  consultation order.
  https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.3982/TE3474
- Kartik, Lee and Suen, *Multi-Sender Disclosure with Costs* (2026): costly
  voluntary multi-sender disclosure, including sequential reporting.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10048
- Hagenbach, Koessler and Perez-Richet, *Certifiable Pre-Play Communication:
  Full Disclosure* (2014): hard evidence and consistency constructions.
  https://edurichet.github.io/papers/HardInfoV13.pdf
- Hu and Sobel, *Simultaneous Versus Sequential Disclosure* (working paper):
  especially relevant remaining timing comparison; its full text was not
  obtained in the originating audit.
  https://econweb.ucsd.edu/~jsobel/working.htm

## Safe novelty position

Do not claim novelty for:
- multi-sender implementability constraints;
- product/rank-one restrictions;
- sequential disclosure as a mechanism;
- correlation/public randomization changing information outcomes;
- information plus monetary incentives.

The narrower candidate is a robust enforcement result under jointly constrained,
analyst-unknown local information conditions, especially when the institution
must choose one sequential protocol that works across the entire admissible
family. This remains a candidate gap, not a certified one.
