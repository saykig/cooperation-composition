# R11 — general sharpness construction gate

19 September 2026. Stable R08 game, equilibrium concept and main research question.

**Completed:** for every d, an explicit rational d-dimensional family needs exactly
d+1 senders in its successful prefix, with dimension-dependent admissible payoffs.
All twenty original dimension-four missing prefixes also have exact rational
witnesses and certified optimal-margin brackets. Fixed-threshold sharpness in
arbitrary families remains open; two precise limitations of the uniform-AM-GM
construction are proved. No computational-hardness or novelty claim.
