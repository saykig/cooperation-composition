# Ukraine: conditional access to coalition imagery

Reviewed 21 September 2026. Labels follow the [methods note](../methods/EMPIRICAL_INFERENCE.md); sources and retrieval limits are in the [manifest](SOURCES.md). No mathematical dependency is assumed.

**INFERENCE — bounded question:** how did US administrative control of the GEGD imagery portal operate during the March 2025 dispute over Ukraine's participation in peace diplomacy? Study 5–12 March, with the preceding diplomatic dispute as context. This is a wartime coalition-access episode, not a DELTA targeting study.

## DELTA lead checked, not conflated

**SOURCE CLAIM:** the MoD's REPMUS 2025 account describes DELTA commanding a multinational red team, routing unmanned-system video through Vezha and coordinating more than 100 platforms. [U1](SOURCES.md#u1) provides exercise evidence only. It does not reveal national disclosure vetoes, a contested release decision, incentive conflict or independently measured decision quality.

**UNKNOWN:** a link between GEGD and DELTA in the March episode. Do not invent one. Access to imagery is also not transfer of targeting authority. Technical standard integration, exercise coordination and wartime bargaining are separate processes.

## Discriminating next evidence

Build a daily chronology with separate columns for imagery accounts, other intelligence, weapons assistance and diplomatic positions. Retain partial exceptions rather than code a universal blackout. Seek an authenticated dated access notice and a public participant account explicitly separating intelligence from weapons pressure. If only the bundled policy change is observable, retain the paper as an institutional account of conditional access; abandon claims that intelligence pressure caused concessions or casualties.
