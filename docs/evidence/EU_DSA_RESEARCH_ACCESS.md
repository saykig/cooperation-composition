# Additional European candidate: X researcher data access

Reviewed 21 September 2026. Labels follow the [methods note](../methods/EMPIRICAL_INFERENCE.md); sources and retrieval limits are in the [manifest](SOURCES.md). No mathematical dependency is assumed.

**INFERENCE — recommended case:** time-sensitive researcher access under DSA Article 40(12), comparing DRI's German-election litigation in 2025 with its Hungarian-election litigation in 2026. The dependent outcome is usable research access before the research window closes, not election results or reduced disinformation. This candidate emerged from searching for actual disclosure disputes beyond GPAI rules.

## Evidence ledger and a serious rival

**FACT:** the May 2025 judgment's urgency reasoning attributes the failed interim claim to delayed pursuit by the applicants. This is evidence against telling a simple story in which all lost time is attributable to X. The original German record is hosted by GFF. [D2](SOURCES.md#d2), PDF pp. 11–13.

**FACT:** the February 2026 court record specifies public tweet, media and interaction data. **SOURCE CLAIM:** the litigators describe quota-free API access ordered through 30 June 2026 and more prompt deadlines in the second episode. Check the original operative order before quoting its exact terms in a manuscript. [D3](SOURCES.md#d3), [D5](SOURCES.md#d5).

**SOURCE CLAIM:** DRI's later public annual-report post says X granted access. This advances the case beyond a court-order announcement, but the post does not establish when usable data arrived or whether the full requested window was covered. Exact post publication date remains unresolved. [D8](SOURCES.md#d8).

**FACT:** the Commission announced a EUR 120 million fine covering three transparency infringements in December 2025. **SOURCE CLAIM:** its account identifies researcher-access barriers. The July 2026 action-plan update concerns advertising-repository changes; it cannot stand in for DRI delivery evidence. [D6](SOURCES.md#d6), [D7](SOURCES.md#d7).

**FACT:** Article 40(12) concerns publicly accessible data and eligibility conditions; Article 40(4) is a different coordinator-mediated route. Do not copy journalistic shorthand about “vetted researchers” into a claim that the two legal routes are identical. [D1](SOURCES.md#d1).

## Why this earns the next attack

The public record contains the same requester and platform in successive disputes, a genuine adverse result, a procedural adaptation and a reported access grant. That is a stronger starting point for testing rivals than a general transparency index or military integration announcement. It still does not establish deliberate delay, comprehensive compliance or an effect on elections. The next attack must test delivery timing against applicant timing and parallel Commission pressure; see the [research protocol](../methods/EMPIRICAL_INFERENCE.md).
