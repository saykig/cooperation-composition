## A bounded chronology, with allegations preserved

**SOURCE CLAIM:** under the indictment account reproduced in HelHO:2026:9, authorities asked about anchor location at 15:20 on 25 December 2024 after learning of Estlink 2 damage. The response allegedly described both anchors as raised and secured; further cable damage allegedly followed. The court expressly separates jurisdiction analysis from guilt. [N7](SOURCES.md#n7).

**FACT:** the published record documents a 2025 jurisdiction dismissal, a prosecution appeal, and the August 2026 reversal. The appellate notice says the decision is not final and may be appealed with leave. A finding of jurisdiction is neither a finding of sabotage nor proof of a strategic motive. [N5](SOURCES.md#n5), [N6](SOURCES.md#n6), [N8](SOURCES.md#n8).

**SOURCE CLAIM:** Warden's January 2025 launch account describes earlier trials and a risk-warning route. It postdates the Christmas incident: do not claim it generated the original Eagle S intervention. The January ministerial statement says warnings were being sent, but provides no matched alert/action records. [N2](SOURCES.md#n2), [N3](SOURCES.md#n3).

**SOURCE CLAIM:** ACT assigns CMRE the technical lead and Allied Maritime Command operational integration for X-Arctic; initial Iceland trials occurred in June/July 2026. This does not establish sharing incentives, classification decisions or deterrence. [N1](SOURCES.md#n1).
