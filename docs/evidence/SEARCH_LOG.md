## Source selection and dependence

The retained [manifest](SOURCES.md) contains sources used in the evidence maps. Discovery-only Reddit, Wikipedia, aggregators and generic compliance advice are not treated as supporting evidence. Reuters reprints share one origin. LIGA and Kyiv Independent accounts of GEGD restoration repeat Militarnyi; they are not independent confirmations. AP and Reuters are independent newsrooms, but quoted government/company sources overlap. OSW is a publicly funded Polish analytical institution, not an operational auditor. Recorded Future News belongs to a cybersecurity business. FMTI is independent empirical research about public disclosure, not a regulator's assessment.

TechPolicy.Press is the venue for a litigant-authored account: authorship, not the host's reputation, determines independence. DRI's own grant report is useful primary testimony with an interest in presenting its litigation positively. German judgments hosted by GFF are court artifacts; GFF's commentary and translation are different evidentiary layers.

## Access and verification limits

The linked GPAI Transparency chapter PDF did not load; no detailed deadline rule was adopted from secondary descriptions. The 2026 German judgment's first page did not yield complete text; the exact quota/end-date wording is attributed to the participant account rather than represented as an independently checked translation. Other relevant reasoning and data categories were readable. Finnish and Ukrainian passages were paraphrased; manuscript quotations require original-language checking.

No confidential systems were accessed. No requests, emails, interviews or purchases were made. No automated tests of provider response or platform data access occurred. Current pages are reviewed as retrieved; the repository stores citations and structured assessments, not immutable copies of all external pages. Null dates and unknown outcomes remain unresolved rather than estimated.
