# What this evidence can support

21 September 2026. A comparative scoping design and the protocol for the next empirical attack. This is a methods note, not a causal-effect estimate.

## Claim vocabulary

- **FACT:** a directly inspectable documentary feature, institutional act or sufficiently corroborated occurrence. The existence of a judgment is a fact; its recitation of an allegation does not make the allegation true.
- **SOURCE CLAIM:** an attributed assertion whose substance has not been independently established here, including official performance claims and participant reports.
- **INFERENCE:** an interpretation supported by identified observations but not directly observed.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** a proposed explanation with a discriminating test still required.
- **UNKNOWN:** a material gap. Missing public evidence is neither evidence of absence nor evidence of concealment.

A legal rule describes an obligation. An announcement describes a claim or commitment. An order records an institutional intervention. Delivery records behaviour. Usability records whether that behaviour supplied the information needed for a decision. These must occupy separate fields.

## Comparative design

The [four evidence maps](../paper/CASE_SELECTION.md) use a common set of questions, but their outcomes are not commensurable observations for a pooled regression. The selection is purposive and evidence-led. It favours a feasible MA process study, not a representative sample of Europe or a success-only sample. Include unsuccessful requests and adverse judgments.

The Ukraine episode can expose access conditionality but bundles imagery, other intelligence, aid and diplomacy. GPAI has observable documentation architecture but lacks a retrieved request-to-decision chain. Nordic Warden lacks linked public alert/action records; Eagle S narrows that candidate to disputed information and jurisdiction, with intent unproven. DRI/X offers successive requests and court records, including failure and a reported later grant. These are reasons for research priority, not statistical findings.

## Next empirical attack: DRI/X delivery and timing

**Working question:** when does a legally enforceable right to public platform data become usable access in time for election-risk research?

**Unit:** a single request and its response, litigation and delivery sequence. Begin with DRI's German-election and Hungarian-election episodes. Keep the Commission proceeding as a separate timeline rather than another observation of the same treatment.

The initial [event ledger](../evidence/DSA_REQUEST_EVENTS.csv) records retrieved events and leaves unknown delivery dates blank. Extend it with request date, requested fields/time span, claimed eligibility, provider response and reasons, applicant follow-up, filing date, court jurisdiction/urgency decision, relief/service date, first successful retrieval, scope/quotas, end of research window, source and claim status. Code unknown delivery dates as missing, not as zero delay. Preserve who reported each event and whether the original record is available.

| Explanation | Evidence that would favour it | Evidence that would weaken it |
|---|---|---|
| Credible judicial relief changes access | Dated provider response or successful retrieval immediately after service, explicitly tied to the order | Grant predates relief; unchanged access despite order |
| Applicant procedural adaptation | Earlier applications, explicit deadlines and prompt filing explain urgency findings | Comparable applicant timing but a different result under otherwise similar procedure |
| Legitimate technical/privacy constraints | Specific documented limitation, consistent application and workable alternative | Changing reasons without changed request; requested fields already available through comparable channels |
| Parallel regulatory pressure | Provider-wide access changes timed to Commission action, including non-litigants | Only the litigating applicant receives relief-specific access |

These are qualitative discriminating observations, not a promise that two episodes identify causation. Preserve the possibility of several mechanisms operating together. Court arguments reveal stated positions and legal findings, not private preferences. A funding relationship is relevant to source independence; it does not itself disprove research findings.

**Next deliverable:** an audited request-to-delivery chronology and one sample/schema comparison between what was ordered and what arrived. Start from the judgments, then retrieve public DRI outputs, access notifications or enforcement records. The current corpus has a participant grant claim but no independently inspected delivery log. No contacts, applications, purchases or interviews were made during this pass.

**Decision rule:** retain the recommended case if at least one delivery record identifies timing and useful scope, and the failed episode's chronology can be reconstructed. If delivery remains inaccessible, narrow the paper to how procedural timing and jurisdiction condition access remedies; do not describe successful behavioural enforcement. If the record only supports rival institutional interpretations, state that limit rather than choose a winner. Revisit Eagle S if communications or trial exhibits become publicly inspectable.

## Limits and safeguards for inference

Maintain a source-dependence graph: Reuters syndications count once, repeated official statements do not become independent measurements, and litigators writing in an independent outlet remain participant sources. Translated judgments require checking the original before manuscript quotation. Extract offence allegations separately from findings and operative dispositions. Later appeal decisions can supersede older jurisdiction claims without establishing guilt.

Do not infer effects on ceasefire acceptance, casualties, election integrity or deterrence from the selected access outcomes. No mathematical import is needed for these claims. No simulation is warranted: a model of delay cannot establish that an actor strategically delayed, or that a real data package arrived.
